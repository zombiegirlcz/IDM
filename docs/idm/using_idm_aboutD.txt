 About Dialog
 "About Internet Download Manager" dialog shows detailed information about program version. Here you can send a message to IDM support service, go to IDM home page, start the registration process or check for the availability of a newer version of IDM.
 Also here you can see to whom IDM is registered and first 7 symbols of its serial number.
